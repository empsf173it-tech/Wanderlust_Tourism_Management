Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a full refund if you cancel your booking at least 48 hours before the trip start date. Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a partial refund depending on the package. Please contact our support team for specific details regarding your booking.

Flights are generally not included unless specifically mentioned in the package details. However, we can help you find the best flight deals to complement your travel package. Just let us know your preferred dates and departure city!

We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance for all our trips. It covers unexpected events such as medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage. We can recommend trusted insurance providers when you book with us.

Simply fill out the contact form above or give us a call, and one of our travel experts will work with you to create a personalized itinerary tailored to your interests, budget, and travel style. We love crafting unique adventures!
